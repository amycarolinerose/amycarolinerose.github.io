I am currently applying to Ph.D. programs in economics and finance in the 2026-2027 cycle. My research interests lie at the intersection of financial intermediation, monetary policy, and household finance. 

In my current role, I work as a pre-doc for Victoria Ivashina at Harvard Business School. I support her on-going research related to private equity and private credit. Previously I was a research assistant at the Federal Reserve Board of Governors in a section called [Money Market Analysis](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/mamma-staff.htm), where I supported research and policy work related to monetary policy implementation. I attended Wellesley College, a historically women's college in Massachusetts, where I studied economics and mathematics. 

In my spare time, I'm an avid crafter. I particularly enjoy sewing and embroidery, but also crochet and knit.
